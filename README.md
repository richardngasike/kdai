## 📖 About Us

**Kimunyak Differently Abled Initiative (KDAI)** is a registered Community-Based Organization operating primarily in Samburu County, Kenya.

KDAI works to promote the rights, inclusion, dignity, and well-being of **People with Disabilities (PWDs)**, with a focus on women, men, youth, and children.

Through a rights-based and people-centered approach, KDAI implements programs in advocacy, climate action, capacity building, mental health, digital inclusion, sustainable livelihoods, education, healthcare, and social inclusion. We also support children with disabilities to access education and, where necessary, medical services including referrals, corrective surgeries, physiotherapy, and rehabilitation.

---

### 🎯 Vision

> To create an inclusive, equitable, and resilient society where People with Disabilities thrive and actively participate in decision-making.

### 🚀 Mission

> To empower PWDs through advocacy, capacity building, and sustainable development initiatives that promote inclusion, climate resilience, education, healthcare, and mental well-being.

## 🎯 Core Objectives

- [x] Amplify the voices of PWDs in leadership, governance, and decision-making.
- [x] Build community resilience to climate change and promote environmental sustainability.
- [x] Promote mental health and psychosocial well-being among PWDs, children, and their families.
- [x] Advance gender equity, inclusion, and access to Sexual and Reproductive Health and Rights (SRHR).

- [x] Promote sustainable livelihoods and economic empowerment for PWDs.

## 🧭 Strategic Focus Areas

### 1. ⚖️ Inclusivity, Human Rights, and SRHR Advocacy
KDAI advocates for the rights and meaningful participation of PWDs in governance and community development. We promote gender equality, access to SRHR information and services, land rights, and participation in decision-making.

### 2. 🌱 Climate Action and Environmental Sustainability
We promote climate resilience through climate-smart agriculture, water conservation, sustainable land management, environmental awareness, and nature-based solutions. We advocate for the inclusion of PWDs in climate action and climate-related decision-making.

### 3. 💻 Digital Inclusion and Capacity Building
We empower PWDs with digital literacy, leadership, advocacy, and other skills that enable them to access information, services, education, employment, and development opportunities.

### 4. 💼 Sustainable Livelihoods and Economic Empowerment
KDAI promotes economic resilience through skills development, climate-smart agriculture, entrepreneurship, sustainable livelihoods, and equitable access to land, resources, markets, and opportunities for PWDs.

### 5. 🎓 Education, Medical Support, and Rehabilitation
KDAI supports children with disabilities to access education and overcome barriers that affect their participation in school. We also identify children who require specialized medical support and facilitate access to appropriate healthcare, including corrective surgeries, physiotherapy, rehabilitation, and medical referrals, with the aim of improving their mobility, independence, dignity, and quality of life.

### 6. 🧠 Mental Health and Psychosocial Well-Being
We promote mental health awareness, psychosocial support, counseling referrals, and inclusive communities that reduce stigma, discrimination, and isolation among PWDs and their families.

### 7. ⚽ Sports and Healthy Practices
KDAI promotes inclusive sports, games, recreation, and healthy lifestyles as tools for physical well-being, confidence, talent development, social inclusion, and community participation.

## 🤝 Our Approach

KDAI uses a **rights-based, disability-led, people-centered, and community-driven approach**.

We work with:
- PWDs, children and their families, caregivers
- Community leaders, schools, healthcare providers
- Government institutions and development partners

To create sustainable and inclusive solutions.

> **We believe that disability should never be a barrier to education, healthcare, economic opportunity, leadership, dignity, or full participation in society.**
